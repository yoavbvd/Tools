# OSCP Practice Resources

> Status and offerings change. Confirm current details on each provider's site.

## Official
- **OffSec PEN-200 course and labs** — the official OSCP preparation path.
- **OffSec Proving Grounds (Practice)** — machines in the OSCP style.

## Community practice platforms
- **Hack The Box** — machines and the "OSCP-like" community lists.
- **TryHackMe** — guided rooms, good for building fundamentals.
- **VulnHub** — downloadable vulnerable VMs for offline practice.

## Reading and references
- **TJnull / NetSecFocus OSCP-like machine list** — a widely used practice set (verify the current version).
- **GTFOBins** and **LOLBAS** — privilege-escalation and living-off-the-land references.
- **HackTricks** — broad methodology reference (study it, do not use AI chatbots during the exam).
- **PayloadsAllTheThings** — technique and payload reference.

## Habit
- Practice without Metasploit on most boxes, since it is restricted in the exam.
- Practice writing a short report for each machine you solve.
